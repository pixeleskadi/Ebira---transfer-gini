# Ebira v1 — release scope

Updated 5 October 2026. Confirmed product direction; unresolved acceptance details remain open.

## November 7 release

Free consumer early access for India, with native Windows and Android phone/tablet apps. Direct download is the initial proposed distribution route and must be validated. The product must access authorised local files directly; a browser-only product is outside this scope.

## Transfer functionality

- Local wireless sharing, with the exact Wi-Fi/hotspot/direct capability matrix to be validated.
- Sequential and selectable round-robin recipient scheduling, one active recipient at a time; switching granularity pending.
- Session approval covers additional files.
- Dedicated receive folders by file type; custom folders are later work.
- Encrypted transfers, integrity verification and interruption recovery.
- Screen-lock continuation and app-restart recovery tested against documented OS limitations.
- Requested 60 GB capacity: per-file/per-batch and GB/GiB definition pending.
- Mandatory 1 Gbps of actual file delivery on tested certified configurations, potentially including hotspot setups. No universal speed guarantee. Bluetooth cannot carry 1 Gbps payloads.

## Follow-on product

Paid Apple-compatible consumer release for the US is desired within November 2026, conditional on readiness; it is not a committed date. macOS, iOS and iPadOS require physical-device validation and legitimate distribution. Business identities/workspaces, managed devices, audit exports and central deployment are deferred beyond the initial consumer release. Office hierarchies, roles, branding, organisation policies and Linux remain later work.

## Hardware

Available: Lenovo Slim 5i/Core Ultra 5 125H, CMF Phone 1, Samsung Tab S9, 15-inch M4 MacBook Air and iPad Air. Exact iPad generation, OS versions and regular Apple-device access are pending. No iPhone is currently available; iPhone certification requires physical testing.

## Security, data and pricing

Encryption, file integrity and reliable recovery are essential launch requirements. Final pairing, credential storage, session lifecycle, cleanup and metadata retention criteria remain to be approved. Only transfer-metadata storage is requested; local key/resume persistence needs explicit definition. No server storage of file contents is approved. The first consumer release is free; paid follow-on pricing and purchase mechanisms are undecided.

## Ready for release

Approved platform/file tests, certified end-to-end performance, integrity/recovery and screen-lock tests, resolved release-blocking defects, legitimate installation/distribution, truthful limits and working onboarding/support must be evidenced. Exact numeric reliability gates and test matrix remain open. Apple follow-on and B2B tasks do not block initial Windows/Android early access. Store approval is required only for a channel being launched. The date alone cannot establish readiness.

## Change control

Feature additions closed on 5 October 2026. Open baseline decisions must be resolved explicitly; new ideas enter the later backlog. No silent additions or claims that planned functionality already exists.
